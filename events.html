Flagship Event

Vishlesh

Vishlesh is R-Squared's flagship analytics conclave — a stage where students, industry leaders, and academic experts come together for stimulating discussions on where data and decision-making meet.

Each edition brings panel discussions, keynote sessions, and case-based challenges designed to expose participants to real analytics problems and the people solving them professionally.

Annual Fest

Ethos Competitions

Ethos is IIM Sambalpur's annual flagship fest, and R-Squared owns the analytics track — running competitions that bring in participants from across the country to solve real, data-driven case challenges.

These competitions are a chance to apply analytical thinking under time pressure, compete for recognition, and connect with like-minded problem-solvers from other campuses.

🏆

What to expect

  • — Real-world, data-driven case problems
  • — Open to participants across B-schools
  • — Recognition and prizes for top teams
  • — Hosted during Ethos, IIM Sambalpur's annual fest
