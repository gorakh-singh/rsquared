About the club

Unlocking the mysteries of data

R-Squared is the Analytics Club of IIM Sambalpur — a passport into the world of analytics for every curious mind on campus.

Our story

Learning by doing

Welcome to the world of analytics, where data is the fuel that drives progress! R-Squared is your passport to unlocking the mysteries of data analysis and exploring the infinite possibilities that lie within.

Our club is a vibrant community of enthusiastic individuals united by a shared passion for analytics. We believe in learning by doing — that's why we organize exciting events like our flagship analytics conclave, Vishlesh, where students engage in stimulating discussions with industry leaders and academic experts.

Our executive committee is made up of passionate students dedicated to promoting the field of analytics within the institute and beyond.

Our Mission

To build a community where every student — regardless of background — can develop real, applicable analytics skills through hands-on experience.

Flagship Event

Vishlesh

Vishlesh is R-Squared's flagship analytics conclave — a stage where students, industry leaders, and academic experts come together for stimulating discussions on where data and decision-making meet.

Each edition brings panel discussions, keynote sessions, and case-based challenges designed to expose participants to real analytics problems and the people solving them professionally.

Annual Fest

Ethos Competitions

Ethos is IIM Sambalpur's annual flagship fest, and R-Squared owns the analytics track — running competitions that bring in participants from across the country to solve real, data-driven case challenges.

These competitions are a chance to apply analytical thinking under time pressure, compete for recognition, and connect with like-minded problem-solvers from other campuses.

Why analytics, why now?

Every industry — from finance to healthcare to retail — now runs on data. Understanding how to read, question, and model that data is quickly becoming a baseline skill for every business leader.

R-Squared exists to close that gap early, giving IIM Sambalpur students hands-on exposure long before they enter the workforce.
