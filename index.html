Who we are

Your passport into the world of analytics

Data is the fuel that drives progress — and R-Squared is where IIM Sambalpur's brightest minds learn to harness it. We're a vibrant community united by a shared passion for analytics and a belief in learning by doing.

From our flagship conclave Vishlesh to national-level competitions, guest lectures, and certification programs, we exist to turn curiosity about data into real, applicable skill.
